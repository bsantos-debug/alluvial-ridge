\subsection{Completed first-flood deposition-threshold sensitivity}
This comparison varies the assumed critical deposition stress $\tau_c$
from a 0.1 Pa baseline to 0.05 and 0.2 Pa. The silt and clay settling
rates contain the factor $\max(0,1-\tau_b/\tau_c)$, where $\tau_b$
is bed shear stress. At fixed shear stress and concentration, lowering
$\tau_c$ suppresses settling; raising it enhances settling where the
shear reduction is active. Sand settling has no shear reduction.
All three cases use the corrected executable, configuration
11, 250 m floodplain grid, 250 m initial channel spacing, CFL limit
0.4, and one complete 20-day hydrograph. All other numerical parameters
agree. All three recorded processes exit successfully.
