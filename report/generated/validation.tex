The retained development validation record reports passing component and
coupled tests. The
lake-at-rest kernel error is $6.76\times10^{-15}$ after 1000 steps.
Analytical settling conserves suspended plus deposited mass, and periodic
sediment diffusion errors decrease from $1.04\times10^{-5}$ to
$3.04\times10^{-6}$ to $7.61\times10^{-7}$ under successive refinement.
Migration tests verify curvature, the upstream convolution, symmetry,
cutoff length accounting, and finite evolution over 580 migration steps.
These are tests of the migration component, not 580 coupled flood runs.

Coupled tests verify reflecting boundaries, two-way class and water
exchange, remapping with and without aggradation, and the complete
suspension--bed--bank-reservoir ledger. A localized suspended plume remains
nonnegative through 150 macro steps. Its water and sediment budget
residuals are approximately $4\times10^{-9}$ m$^3$ and
$4\times10^{-11}$ kg. A steady normal-channel test has maximum relative
depth errors 0.179\% and 0.0843\% at 250 and 125 m section spacing.
The imposed reservoir-release waveform error decreases from 0.004915
to 0.002461 when the CFL coefficient is halved. Positive integration
remainders remain in the reservoir; injected plus remaining mass is
conserved.

A two-flood short-domain checkpoint test reproduces uninterrupted raster
output, channel coordinates, and the final checkpoint byte-for-byte.
Changed checkpoint parameters are rejected, and diagnostic rows written
after the last valid checkpoint are regenerated. These checks establish
restart behavior for the tested case.

This development summary predates completion of the full-domain first-flood
cases and is not a live record of their status. The separately archived
findings from corrected rebuild 002 appear in
Section~\ref{sec:first-flood-validation}, including completed 250 m and 500 m
sensitivity cases and the captured first flood of all 31 production cases.
The component tests summarized here do not establish grid independence or
long-term quantitative agreement for those production configurations.
Exact commands and logs are in \texttt{validation/} and
\texttt{docs/numerical-validation.md}.
